# Ross Group Corporate Brand System — Ross PDF Editor

## Source reference
This specification is derived from the supplied Ross Group Holdings, Inc. corporate family / brand-board reference for the PDF Editor product.

## Corporate hierarchy
- Parent Corporation: Ross Group Holdings, Inc.
- Flagship division/product: Ross PDF Editor
- Product descriptor: Powerful. Secure. Professional.
- Corporate positioning: One corporate family. Unlimited solutions.
- Product purpose: intelligent document editing, conversion, OCR, forms, review, security, automation and AI assistance.

## Brand palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy Blue | #0B1D3A | primary brand field, navigation, headers |
| Gold | #D4AF37 | premium accents, CTAs, dividers, icons |
| Silver | #C0C0C0 | secondary metallic accents, selected marks |
| Tan | #E7D6B5 | warm background surfaces and premium print areas |
| Cream | #F6EFE4 | page background and stationery surfaces |

## Typography
- Display: Playfair Display / serif editorial treatment for brand-facing marketing headings.
- Interface: Inter / system sans for product UI, labels, forms and operational dashboards.
- Use uppercase tracking for product labels and short navigation descriptors.

## Product logo rules
- Use `apps/web/public/assets/ross-pdf-editor-mark.svg` for compact product identity.
- Use `apps/web/public/assets/ross-group-mark.svg` for parent-company/corporate-family contexts.
- Preserve clear space around both marks.
- Do not distort, recolor, rotate, or place the marks on low-contrast backgrounds.
- The product logo should be the global home navigation target.

## Application surfaces
The Ross PDF Editor brand system applies consistently to:
- Public homepage and marketing pages
- Access gate / sign in / sign up / recovery
- Onboarding
- Workspace and editor
- Documents
- Forms
- OCR
- Pages
- Review and collaboration
- Accessibility
- Security
- Conversions
- Automations
- Templates
- Account
- Billing
- Administration
- Developer console
- AI Assist
- Email, invoices, exports and print-ready documents

## Interaction standard
Every persistent product logo must link to `/` with an accessible name such as `Ross PDF Editor home`. Keyboard focus must be visible. Navigation should never use a decorative logo as the sole unlabeled control.

## AI branding
The AI persona is `ANDREAA CHAN'NEL IMITATION`, presented explicitly as an AI assistant and not as the human individual. The AI layer should use the Ross PDF Editor palette and must not imply human authorship of AI-generated output.

## IP and authenticity
Copyright, trademarks and ownership notices should reflect the actual corporate ownership and registrations. Government marks, official seals and certification badges must never be fabricated or implied.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Logo renders crisply at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes.
2. Clicking the product logo routes to `/` from every application surface.
3. Favicon uses the product mark.
4. Navy/gold/silver/tan/cream tokens are centralized in the design system.
5. Contrast and keyboard focus remain accessible.
6. Parent-company references appear only where contractually appropriate.
